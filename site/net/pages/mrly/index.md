## MrlyMath

I was designing a new logo for MrlyProd (2017). I was looking for a written mark that when collapsed would become a figurative mark. That turned out to be a square with four holes in it. Which turned out to be the first iteration of the _next_ Sierpinski Carpet. I had no idea what fractals were at the time. Now I know...

## MrlyLogo

The MrlyLogo has 21 fill squares and 4 void squares for a total of 25 grid squares in a 5×5 grid. It generalizes to one (tree), two (carpet) and three (sponge) dimensions. It resembles the ancient [Sun Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_cross).
