# Shipping

Who prints, who ships, and how long it takes.

Printful prints every item on demand and ships it from the facility closest to you. Production takes a few business days before the parcel moves, and delivery time depends on the carrier and the destination country.

Tracking arrives by email from Shopify once the parcel leaves. Questions about a parcel already on its way go to [Printful support](https://help.printful.com).
