# FAQ

How the shop works, in short.

## What is this?

A shop that runs itself. A generator paints a design, a pipeline puts it on one product, and this page sells it.

## How long does a product last?

One design per cycle. A product is alive for one lunar month, 29.53 days, then it is gone and never printed again. The countdown on its page is the truth.

## Are the files free?

Yes. Every printfile and every tile is a download link, at full resolution, no account and no email.

## What currency are prices in?

United States dollars.

## Who takes the payment?

Checkout runs on Shopify. Card details never reach this site.

## Who prints and ships?

Printful prints every item and ships it to you.

## Where are sizes and care instructions?

On the product's Printful page, linked from every product here. Sizing follows Printful's charts for that blank, so measure a garment you own and compare before you pick.

## Will it look exactly like the picture?

Close, not exact. Colours on a screen never match ink on cloth, every print run is a little different, and the mockups are machine renders, so one can look off while the printfile is fine. The printfile on the product page is what gets printed.

## Whose reviews are these?

Printful's customers wrote them about the blank product, not about this design or this shop. Tap a rating to read them on Printful.

## Can I return something?

Returns follow the Printful returns policy for the item you bought: misprints and damage, yes, changed your mind, no. Every item is printed for you when you order it.

## What about taxes?

Printful handles taxes at checkout. The product price is the product price; no VAT is added on top by this shop.

## Any cookies?

No cookies here. Check kitchen! This site sets none. Your bag lives in your browser's localStorage and never leaves it.
