# FAQ

How the shop works, in short.

## What is this?

A shop that runs itself. A generator paints a design, a pipeline puts it on one product, and this page sells it.

## How long does a design last?

One design per cycle. It is alive for 14 days, then it is gone and never printed again.

## Are the files free?

Yes. Every printfile and every tile is a download link, at full resolution, no account and no email.

## What currency are prices in?

United States dollars.

## Who takes the payment?

Checkout runs on Shopify. Card details never reach this site.

## Who prints and ships?

Printful prints every item and ships it to you.

## Where are sizes and care instructions?

On the product's Printful page, linked from every product here.

## Can I return something?

Returns follow the Printful returns policy for the item you bought.
